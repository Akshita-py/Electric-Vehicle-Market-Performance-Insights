# Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights

## 📌 About the Project

Electric vehicles are becoming an important part of the automobile industry. This project explores the electric vehicle (EV) market using Tableau Desktop to understand sales performance across manufacturers, vehicle categories, model years, and countries.

The main goal is to turn EV market data into clear and useful visualizations that make it easier to understand sales patterns and compare different parts of the market.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand EV sales performance across different manufacturers.
- Compare sales across various vehicle categories.
- Explore changes in EV sales over different model years.
- Analyze sales across different countries.
- Present the findings through clear visualizations and a Tableau dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Tableau Desktop** — For creating charts and dashboards.
- **CSV Datasets** — For storing and analyzing EV market data.
- **GitHub** — For maintaining the project files and documentation.

## 📂 Dataset Description

This project includes two datasets:

- `ev_market_train.csv`
- `ev_market_test.csv`

The datasets contain information about EV manufacturers, vehicle categories, annual sales, model years, battery specifications, driving range, vehicle prices, customer ratings, manufacturing countries, and sustainability metrics.

The current Tableau analysis uses the train dataset. The train and test datasets are kept separate and are not combined.

## 📊 Data Visualizations

Four visualizations have been worked on as part of this project:

### 1. Sales by Manufacturer — Bar Chart
This chart compares annual EV sales across manufacturers and helps show differences in their sales performance.

### 2. Sales by Category — Donut Chart
This chart shows how EV sales are distributed across different vehicle categories, making it easier to compare their contributions to total sales.

### 3. EV Sales Trend — Line Chart
This chart displays EV sales across model years to help explore how sales change over time.

### 4. Sales by Country — Country Plot
This visualization presents EV sales across countries and helps explore geographical differences in sales performance.

### 5. Annual Sales by Battery Technology — Bar Chart
This visualization compares EV sales across different battery technologies and helps identify which battery types contribute more to overall sales.

### 6. Price vs. Annual Sales — Scatter Plot
This chart explores the relationship between vehicle prices and annual sales, helping examine how sales vary across different price levels.

### 7. EV Sales by Manufacturer — Treemap
This visualization represents sales contributions from different manufacturers using rectangle sizes, making it easier to compare their relative performance.
